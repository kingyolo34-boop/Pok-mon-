# Download and test Pokémon Fallen Ashe's GBA build

## Where the game comes from
GitHub's "Build Fallen Ashe GBA (starter test)" workflow checks out a pinned pokeemerald-expansion source version, applies our source changes, installs the ARM compiler, and builds a GBA ROM. Download only from a run marked Success.

## On Android
1. Open this repository's Actions tab.
2. Select "Build Fallen Ashe GBA (starter test)".
3. Open the latest successful run.
4. Under Artifacts download "Fallen-Ashe-GBA-Starter-Test".
5. Extract the ZIP to get pokeemerald.gba.
6. In Pizza Boy A Pro or My Boy!, load pokeemerald.gba and start a New Game.
7. At Birch's starter selection look for Verdrake (Grass), Aqualye (Water), and Embergryph (Fire).

## What this FIRST source test includes
- Emerald Expansion's original battles, movement and saves.
- Our nine starter names, types, stats and level evolutions, in temporary Hoenn starter species slots.
- The original Emerald map, menus, and ORIGINAL starter battle sprites as temporary placeholders.

## NOT integrated yet
- Custom starter sprite sheets
- Burning Cinderfall and the scripted Rook opening
- Wren's verified 2× EXP Blissey training system
- Legendary companion species Deku and Asta
- Shadowvile, Smokerdile and Veylith

This is a compiler/mechanics smoke test, not a finished Fallen Ashe chapter. Keep your previous working ROM backed up and use a fresh save. Do not upload commercial ROM or BIOS files to this public repository.
